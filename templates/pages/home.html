A lasting finish.
Done properly.

A good paint job is mostly preparation. Every vehicle goes through the same four steps, so the paint looks even and stands up to Ghana's heat and dust.

  1. Inspect: we look over the vehicle with you and agree on the work, paint and price.
  2. Prepare: we strip, sand and treat any rust so the surface is clean and sound.
  3. Prime and spray: anti-corrosion primer, an even base coat and a durable clear coat.
  4. Finish: oven baking, machine polishing and a final check before you collect your vehicle.
