01 About us

One workshop.
Every finish.

Baah Auto Spraying is a vehicle spraying and finishing workshop based in the Light Industrial Area of Community 9, Tema. We handle everything from minor scratch repairs to complete body resprays, for cars and motorbikes alike.

Bring your vehicle to the workshop and we will inspect it with you and give you a clear, honest assessment of the work required.

02 The founder

Meet
Mr. Mark Kwesi Baah.

Founder and Workshop Lead

Mr. Mark Kwesi Baah founded Baah Auto Spraying and continues to lead the day-to-day paint and spray work at our Community 9 workshop. When you visit, you speak with him directly about your vehicle's condition, your choice of paint and the expected turnaround time.

03 Our story

Built on
preparation.

A lasting finish starts with proper preparation. Every job follows the same careful process: we strip and prepare the surface, apply a durable primer, spray, and finish with a machine polish.

That way the paint looks even and stands up to Ghana's heat and dust, long after the car has left the workshop.
